# Style record

Measured from the six figures in [../assets/examples/](../assets/examples/), schematics published in
Nature in 2025 and 2026. Their print widths are assumed: 183 mm for the five that are about 2,100 px
wide and about 120 mm for `process-nanogel.png`. Type and line sizes hold at either column width.
Note task-specific changes in the figure brief, not here.

## Colour

Neutral cells, model blocks and group boxes are pale blue-grey, `#E0E4E9` or `#DADFE6`, with mid
tones `#819DB2` and `#9FB3C0` and dark accent `#6A6F7B`. Text is near black, `#222`. The page is
white.

Each accent hue has one meaning. Organs use muted natural colours, such as a pink brain and an olive
gut, or their category hue.

## Rendering

- Organic forms have fine darker contours with flat tone regions or soft shading, without drop
  shadows.
- Diagram boxes, chips and arrows stay flat.
- People appear as a translucent grey body silhouette, or the organ involved stands in for them.

## Type

- Labels are Arial in sentence case, most in regular weight.
- Panel titles, group headings and selected organ or stage names are bold. Panel letters are bold
  lowercase.
- Body labels are 7 pt and minor annotations no smaller than 5 pt. Panel letters are 8 pt.
- Direction of change goes inline as ↑ or ↓ before the term.

## Connectors

- Lines are 0.5 to 1 pt, never under 0.25 pt. Directed processes use dark lines with filled heads
  about 1.5 mm long.
- An inhibition ends in a flat bar on the step it blocks, before any branch beyond that step.
- A drug or treatment sits on or beside the arrow it acts on.
- A dashed line marks a second kind of relation, such as movement between compartments or an
  uncertain link.
- Labels sit beside their objects, with a fine leader only where proximity is ambiguous. Leaders and
  grouping brackets have no heads.

## Compositions seen

The examples are a hub and spoke (`hub-liver-organs.png`), a progression (`progression-liver.png`),
a tree (`tree-body-diseases.png`), a timeline (`timeline-immunity.png`), lettered panels
(`panels-digital-twin.png`) and a process strip (`process-nanogel.png`). Related items pack tightly,
with clear space between groups.
