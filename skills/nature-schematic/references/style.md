# Style record

Measured on 2026-11-02 from the six figures in [../assets/examples/](../assets/examples/),
schematics published in Nature in 2025 and 2026. Their print size is not recorded: five are about
2,100 px wide, taken as 183 mm, and `process-nanogel.png` as about 120 mm. Type and line sizes are
absolute and hold at either column width. Note task-specific changes in the figure brief, not here.

## Colour

Neutral cells, model blocks and group boxes are pale blue-grey, `#E0E4E9` or `#DADFE6`, with mid
tones `#819DB2` and `#9FB3C0` and dark accent `#6A6F7B`. Text is near black, `#222`. The page is
white.

Accents carry meaning, one hue per meaning. A timeline pairs cyan with orange, a nanogel process
uses blue chains and red antigens, and a disease tree uses red, blue, ochre and purple branches with
a pale tint of each behind its label. Organs use muted natural colours, such as a pink brain, salmon
muscle, yellow pancreas and olive gut, or their category hue.

## Rendering

- Organic forms have fine darker contours with flat tone regions or soft shading. No drop shadows.
- Molecular beads may have darker rims and small highlights.
- Diagram boxes, chips and arrows stay flat.
- People appear as a translucent grey body silhouette, or the organ involved stands in for them.

## Type

- Arial, sentence case, regular weight for most labels. Arial has the ↑ and ↓ glyphs that some
  Helvetica builds lack.
- Bold for panel titles, group headings and selected organ or stage names. Panel letters are bold
  lowercase.
- Body labels are 7 pt and minor annotations no smaller than 5 pt, measured at final size. Panel
  letters are 8 pt.
- Direction of change goes inline as ↑ or ↓ before the term.

## Connectors

- Lines are 0.5 to 1 pt, never under 0.25 pt. Directed processes use dark lines with filled heads
  about 1.5 mm long.
- An inhibition ends in a flat bar on the step it blocks, before any branch beyond that step.
- A drug or treatment sits on or beside the arrow of the process it acts on.
- Dashed lines carry a second meaning, such as movement between compartments or an uncertain link,
  and the legend says which.
- Label leaders and grouping brackets have no heads.
- Tree branches use category colours and rounded bends.
- Timeline stems end in markers, with dashed event lines and open flags.
- Molecules appear as small coloured dots beside a flow arrow, labelled with their class and
  examples.
- Labels sit beside their objects. Use a fine leader only where proximity is ambiguous.

## Compositions seen

- Hub and spoke (`hub-liver-organs.png`): one central cell or organ with inputs around it.
- Progression (`progression-liver.png`): the same organ redrawn per stage.
- Tree (`tree-body-diseases.png`): a body silhouette branching to categories, then diseases.
- Timeline (`timeline-immunity.png`): markers above and below a shared axis.
- Lettered panels (`panels-digital-twin.png`) with pale boxes for model blocks.
- Process strip (`process-nanogel.png`) with leader labels, about 120 mm wide.

Related items pack tightly, with clear space between groups.
