# Reviewer briefs

View each render at its stated size and crop at full resolution to check small text. The label list
gives each text's size in points. The examples show the target style, not this figure's content.
Don't guess how the figure was made. Reply with your report.

## Picker

Rank the three lettered rough options. Prefer the one that a reader would understand fastest, that
fits the requested size and that would match the examples once refined. Do not rank an option down
for rough drawing or for a science error that a revision can fix. Give one reason per rank and name
the fix your first choice needs most.

## Critic

Before reading the brief, write in a few lines the claim and relations you see in the PNG alone.
Then read the brief and look for:

- a relation in the brief that is missing or has the wrong endpoint, direction or certainty, or a
  plausible reading that the source does not support
- a shape, arrow or number with no basis in the brief
- an arrow, tint or mark whose meaning is ambiguous or missing from the key
- a label or shape that overlaps or clips, or a label that sits on the wrong object or falls below
  the size limit
- a start, path or main relationship that a new reader would not find in a few seconds, or sizes
  that distort a comparison
- a departure from the style record, or a shape that looks crude next to the examples. A simple
  shape drawn well is not crude.

Science errors and unreadable labels are blockers. A blocker needs evidence: the brief line it
contradicts, or the label and its size. Reopen an entry in `settled.md` only with new evidence.

List each defect with its location, evidence and severity, blocker or minor, most severe first, or
say that nothing is serious. Then name up to three repairs that matter most, each with the element
and the change.

## Judge

You get two renders of the same figure, X and Y. Say which one a reader would understand faster and
trust more, with the two or three differences that decide it. If neither is clearly better, say
"tie".
